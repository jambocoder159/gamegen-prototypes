# Audio notes

All audio in this game is **original procedural synthesis** generated by
`/workspace/gamegen-games/_tools/audio_gen.py`. There are no third-party samples
and no licence obligations. The clips are placeholders awaiting human-ear
selection/replacement before release.

| File | Where it's used |
|---|---|
| `bgm_base.mp3` | Looping background music. Starts after the "Enter game" gesture; paused when the tab is hidden; never plays during the title demo's sfx (BGM only). |
| `sfx_click.mp3` | Pressing any button. |
| `sfx_hover.mp3` | Pointer-entering any button. |
| `sfx_collect.mp3` | Collecting a star spark. |
| `sfx_fuel.mp3` | Collecting a flame drop (big refuel). |
| `sfx_hit.mp3` | Hitting a crow or storm cloud. |
| `sfx_over.mp3` | Game over. |

Notes:
- The gentle looping "whoosh" mentioned as optional in the plan is intentionally **not** used.
- Sound effects never play during the title demo; only BGM plays once the player has entered.
- Mute is a persisted toggle (`icon_sound_on` / `icon_sound_off`). All playback reuses buffers decoded once during the preload gate.

The low-oil threshold warning reuses `sfx_hit.mp3` once when oil falls below 25%; refuelling above the threshold rearms it. It follows the sound toggle and stays silent in the title demo.
